# 🌍 International SWE / AI / ML Internship & Job Target List

**For:** BTech AI/ML student \| C++ / DSA \| GenAI \| RAG \| AI Agents
\| Full-stack AI\
**Goal:** Find strong-paying international, remote, European, AI,
HFT/quant, and lesser-known opportunities instead of applying only to
FAANG.

> **Important:** "Less competition" is not guaranteed for any company.
> The purpose of this list is to identify companies that are less
> obvious than FAANG, have specialized hiring funnels, international
> offices, strong engineering teams, or remote/distributed models.
>
> **Always verify:** current opening, graduation year, work
> authorization, visa sponsorship, location restrictions, and whether an
> internship accepts students outside the company's home country.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 How to use this list

### Priority system

-   🔴 **Dream / extremely competitive** --- apply anyway, but don't
    depend on these.
-   🟠 **High-value target** --- excellent fit for DSA/AI and worth
    serious networking.
-   🟢 **Opportunity target** --- smaller/specialized companies where
    your projects can help you stand out.
-   🌍 **Remote/distributed** --- remote model exists, but each role can
    have country restrictions.
-   🇪🇺 **Europe** --- particularly useful for international
    internship/job searches.
-   🇮🇳 **India-accessible** --- has India opportunities or an India
    team; useful if overseas sponsorship is difficult.
-   ⚠️ **Check eligibility** --- location/work authorization is
    especially important.

# 12. 🤝 REFERRAL APPROACH

### First connection

Don't immediately ask:

> "Can you refer me?"

Use:

> Hi \[Name\], I'm a BTech AI/ML student currently preparing for SWE/ML
> internships while building GenAI and agentic AI projects. I came
> across your work at \[Company\] and would love to connect and learn
> from your experience.

### After connection

Show genuine interest in their work.

### When you find an actual role

Then ask:

> Hi \[Name\], I found the \[Role\] opening at \[Company\] and it aligns
> closely with my background in \[DSA/AI/ML/etc.\]. I've also been
> building \[specific project\]. If you feel my profile is a fit, would
> you be comfortable referring me for the role? Here's my resume:
> \[link\]. Completely understand if you're unable to.

**A specific job + specific fit + concise resume is much stronger than a
generic referral request.**

# ⚠️ IMPORTANT ELIGIBILITY RULE

Before getting excited about a salary or internship:

### Check these 6 things

-   [ ] Does the company hire interns in my graduation year?
-   [ ] Does the specific role accept international students?
-   [ ] Does it require existing work authorization?
-   [ ] Is visa sponsorship provided?
-   [ ] Is the job actually remote from India?
-   [ ] Is compensation location-adjusted?

For example, ASML's US internship page currently says applicants must be
authorized to work in the United States, while IMC has an India/APAC
student pipeline with engineering internships. So "international
company" does not automatically mean "Indian student can apply from
India."
