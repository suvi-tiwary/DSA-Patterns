## 7. Application workflow

1.  **Apply to the nearest deadline first.** The dates above are as
    checked on 12 Oct 2026; verify each link before spending time on an
    application.
2.  Open the actual employer/event page. Do not rely only on a search
    result or aggregator.
3.  Confirm: year of graduation, current year of study,
    location/remote-country rules, work authorization, duration,
    stipend, and deadline.
4.  Use a role-specific resume. Highlight DSA/C++, Python, FastAPI,
    React, APIs, databases, deployed projects, LLM/RAG/agent work, and
    measurable outcomes only where accurate.
5.  Keep an application log with: role, company, direct URL, deadline,
    date applied, status, follow-up date, and notes.
6.  Never pay a recruiter or share sensitive identity/banking details
    just to apply. If a hackathon charges an entry fee, verify the
    organiser and terms first.
